## 🧠 Ceo-CVE چیست؟

**Ceo-CVE** فقط یه اسکریپت ساده نیست.

## 🧠 What is Ceo-CVE?

**Ceo-CVE** is not just another CVE script.
It's a **weaponized intelligence engine** that rips raw vulnerability data apart and hands you **clean, structured, ready-to-use knowledge** — straight into your terminal.

Instead of drowning in scattered CVE databases, blog posts, and messy NVD pages,
**Ceo-CVE** gives you:

- ✅ **Instant** CVE lookups
- ✅ **Clean** structured output
- ✅ **Zero** bloat, minimal dependencies
- ✅ **Maximum** speed in your workflow

> 💡 *"Know your vulnerabilities before they know you."*
